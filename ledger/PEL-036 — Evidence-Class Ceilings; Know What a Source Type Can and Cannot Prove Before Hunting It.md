# PEL-036 — Evidence-Class Ceilings; Know What a Source Type Can and Cannot Prove Before Hunting It

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Project:** Calla market validation · **Consolidates:** SSP-027, -056, -057, -058, -059 candidates
**Tags:** _evidence quality · buyer evidence · WTP · pain evidence · price patterns_

## Quick Recognition Test
A criterion "passes" on a source whose type could not honestly carry it, or a hunt keeps returning the same thin class.

## Root Cause
Each source class has a hard ceiling:
- Municipal claims lists and treasurer vendor reports are payment-class: vendor plus amount, no product purpose, no acquisition context.
- Federal public-comment voice is overwhelmingly mediated (associations, lawyers), and structured submitter fields are almost always empty.
- Open-text pain search over a comment corpus ranks controversy volume, not compliance pain.
- Bare "burden" is the PRA term of art every ICR notice solicits.
- A dollar-pattern cannot tell a price from a penalty ("$100,000 per violation") or an ICR aggregate cost estimate.
- A regulator's page, or one word in a site's footer nav, can pose as buyer evidence (D-096).
- A done-for-you service price is a ceiling on self-serve willingness to pay (Performance West $499 sank PC-008's market math).

## What NOT to Waste Time On
Hunting a criterion in a class that cannot carry it, or letting a single anchor word decide a criterion.

## Canonical Fix Pattern
- Match the source class to the criterion before hunting:
  - claims lists → spend and durability only
  - comment corpora → voice only with paperwork-vocabulary co-occurrence plus ICR-topic relevance
- Guard numeric patterns against penalty and estimate contexts.
- Read any service price at stage 1 as the WTP ceiling.

## When to Apply This Again
Planning any evidence hunt or reviewing a criterion that passed on an unusual source type.
